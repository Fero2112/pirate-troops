THE GAME

Pirate Troops is a Tactical RPG that mixes point and click immersive storytelling with fun card collection & fighting mechanics. Solving puzzles and mysteries will lead to unlocking new ability cards and unfold the story of the Shortlegs Family!

Sammy Shortlegs is a pirate kid who lives on the island of Tortuga Gorda with his Gramps. He will venture the seas in search of his Father; one of the mightiest pirates ever known, who disappeared under mysterious circumstances. Full of old-school puzzle design, a cast of uncanny characters, and beautiful pixel art.

Pirate Troops is a love letter to RPG games from the SNES and PSX era.

You can also play a short demo of a very early version here!
